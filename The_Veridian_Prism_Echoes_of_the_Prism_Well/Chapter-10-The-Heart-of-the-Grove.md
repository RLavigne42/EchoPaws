# Chapter 10: The Heart of the Grove

They returned to Coordinate Zero beneath the Canopy of Stillness, where the Pillar of Memory rose luminous and the Prism Well shimmered with teal, magenta, and amber light. Echo’s scale-feathers glowed indigo as they synchronized with the pillar’s logic. Paws’ confetti flecks lifted from their fur, floating like tiny sparks in the air.

Echo stood steady, a beacon of wisdom and calm observation. The owl’s indigo eyes held the knowledge of frozen waterfalls, petrified arches, and silver tides. Paws curled beside him, playful and curious even at rest, their soft smirk promising a next leap. Together they balanced strategy and spontaneity, echoing the core truth of their world: wisdom maps the path, imagination brings it to life.

They spoke of the Fractured Wilds beyond the known Prism, where new symbols might be born and unfamiliar colors might rise. Echo would measure the logic of those uncharted edges; Paws would scatter color to reveal the hidden routes. In the glow of the Prism Well, they agreed again to walk side by side.

The Heart of the Grove held them in stillness, but the world beyond called. With one last glance at the Silver Ferns, they stepped forward—Echo’s precision and Paws’ creativity woven into a single, living path.
