# Chapter 6: Petrified Arches

Northward, the Petrified Arches (🏛️) rose like the ribs of ancient giants. White limestone spanned the sky, each arch a remnant of the world’s first Logics. Echo moved with reverence here, his wings folding neatly against his body, symmetry in every step. The arches were navigation markers for Echo’s kin, the Wise Guardians who once charted the Prism by starlight.

Paws trotted beside him, head tilted, curious despite the solemn air. The cat’s playful color splashes glowed softly against the pale stone, creating a living contrast to the rigid geometry. “So these are the bones of order,” Paws whispered, tapping a paw to the limestone and listening for echoes.

Echo paused beneath a towering span, indigo eyes reflecting the intricate linework carved into the stone. “These arches held the world together when color was new,” he said, his voice calm as a winter moon. “They remind us of the cost of certainty.”

Paws flicked their tail, eyes bright with wonder. “And they remind us we can climb higher,” they replied. Together they scaled a ledge, the owl’s precision and the cat’s energy creating a rhythm. From the apex, they saw the whole Prism spread below, a living tapestry of logic and color.
