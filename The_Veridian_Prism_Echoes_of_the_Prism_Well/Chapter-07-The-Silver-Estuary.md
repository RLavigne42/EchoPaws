# Chapter 7: The Silver Estuary

They reached the coast where the Silver Estuary shimmered like liquid memory. The water was mercury-bright, reflecting not the clouds but the past. Every ripple was a story, every wave a recollection. Silver Ferns drifted along the shore, seedlings carried on the wind from the estuary’s breath.

Echo watched the tide with the patience of a library keeper, his indigo gaze absorbing the countless histories held in the water. The owl’s cool-toned plumage matched the estuary’s sheen, and for a moment it seemed as if he had been sculpted from the same luminous silver.

Paws stepped closer, pawpads tingling as the water whispered their name. In the shimmering surface they saw reflections of their own adventures: the leap from the Silver Fern, the rainbow dust of the Chroma-Fields, the stillness of the frozen waterfalls. The cat’s smirk softened into something tender. “So every story returns here,” they murmured.

Echo nodded. “The estuary keeps memory alive, like the Prism Well keeps color alive.” Together they collected a glowing fern frond, its pale light anchoring their path. The estuary taught them that memory is a river, and they were both its guardians and its travelers.
