# Chapter 2: The Prism and the Parable

The Silent Grove whispered with moss and amber light when Paws found a Silver Fern (🌿) perched above a chasm. It glowed pale and inviting, a memory-anchored illusion that hummed with the faintest hint of the Silver Estuary. Paws’ golden eyes sparkled with curiosity, and the smirk on their face dared gravity to keep up.

Echo watched without admonishment, his indigo gaze ancient and observant. He knew the lesson hidden in the fern’s luminous fronds: it was not rooted to earth, but to remembrance. The cat leapt anyway—ginger stripes and rainbow flecks streaking through the air—and the fern dissolved beneath their paws. The fall came swift, a plunge into the grove’s mist.

Echo’s feathers pulsed with a low-frequency hum. Starlight Stillness rippled outward, slowing time as if the world itself inhaled and held its breath. Leaves froze midair; the chasm’s mist suspended into silver beads. Echo glided forward and caught Paws by the scruff, their confetti flecks drifting like stardust in the slowed light.

As they reached a solid limestone ledge, Paws grinned up at Echo, curiosity unbroken. Echo, regal and composed, released the cat with a gentle nod. The parable echoed across the grove: Echo prevented disaster, but Paws prevented Echo from turning into a statue of pure logic. Between them, the Prism’s balance held.
