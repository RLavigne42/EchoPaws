# Echo: Demeanor

## Core Philosophy

Everything about Echo's design is centered and grounded. While Paws is about movement and mess, Echo is about symmetry and precision.

## Character Traits

Echo embodies the "wise" persona through:
- **Symmetry**: Balanced, intentional design
- **Precision**: Every detail has purpose
- **Stillness**: A sense of calm observation
- **Ancient Wisdom**: An ageless quality
- **Ethereal Connection**: A link to the nocturnal and mystical

## Visual Expression

Unlike Paws' playful smirk and explosive energy, Echo presents:
- A composed, knowing presence
- Stillness that suggests deep thought
- Regal dignity without arrogance
- The quiet confidence of one who has witnessed much

## Role

Echo serves as the perfect counterpart to Paws' chaotic energy, representing order, wisdom, and serene understanding. Together, they create a complementary dynamic where youthful enthusiasm balances ancient knowledge.
