# Echo: Plumage and Texture

## Color Palette

Echo's feathers are strictly cool-toned:
- White
- Silver
- Icy blue
- Deep navy

## Chest and Face Feathers

The feathers on the chest and face are layered like delicate scales or crystalline plates, giving Echo a regal, almost armored appearance.

## Design Excellence

This is a masterclass in detail. The layered, scale-like texture creates:
- A sense of protection and strength
- An ethereal, ice-like quality
- Grounded, dignified presence
- Visual symmetry that emphasizes order and precision

The texture work reinforces Echo's wisdom and ancient connection to nocturnal observation.
