# Echo: Facial Features

## Eyes

- **Color**: Deep, hypnotic indigo or "galaxy" blue
- **Expression**: Ancient and observant, reflecting a sense of stillness
- **Contrast**: Unlike Paws' curious gaze, Echo's eyes feel timeless and wise
- **Impression**: Convey deep knowledge and understanding

## Beak

- **Color**: Translucent blue-black hue
- **Position**: Sharp, polished, and neatly tucked beneath facial features
- **Context**: Positioned beneath a "mustache" of soft white down

## Ear Tufts

- **Design**: Swept back and stylized
- **Appearance**: Resembles a crown that frames the face
- **Function**: Adds to the regal, dignified bearing

## Overall Impression

Echo's facial features work together to create an expression of timeless wisdom and ethereal grace. The design emphasizes:
- Ancient knowledge
- Calm observation
- Regal dignity
- Nocturnal mystery
