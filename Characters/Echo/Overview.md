# Echo: The Wise Owl

Echo stands in stark contrast to Paws, serving as the "wise" counterpart. The design is intricate, symmetrical, and suggests a deep connection to the ethereal or the nocturnal.

## Key Characteristics

- **Species**: Owl
- **Personality**: Wise, Observant, Serene
- **Visual Theme**: Cool tones, symmetry, ethereal
- **Energy Level**: Radiating calm, calculated serenity

## Design Philosophy

Everything about Echo's design is centered and grounded. While Paws is about movement and mess, Echo is about symmetry and precision, embodying the "wise" persona.
