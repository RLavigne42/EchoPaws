# Paws: Expression

## The Smirk

Paws has a soft, knowing smirk that perfectly matches the "playful" descriptor. This expression is neither aggressive nor overly innocent—it's knowing, as if Paws is always aware of something amusing happening around them.

## Emotional Resonance

The smirk combines with the massive eyes and colorful design to create an overall impression of:
- Mischievousness
- Confidence
- Joy and wonder
- A hint of chaotic energy

This expression is key to understanding Paws' role as the chaotic, energetic counterpart to Echo's serene wisdom.
