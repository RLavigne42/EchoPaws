# Paws: The Playful and Curious Cat

Paws is designed to embody pure, youthful energy. This avatar feels less like a standard house cat and more like a creature born from a vibrant art studio.

## Key Characteristics

- **Species**: Cat
- **Personality**: Playful, Curious, Energetic
- **Visual Theme**: Chaotic color, vibrant, rebellious
- **Energy Level**: Bursting with chaotic color
