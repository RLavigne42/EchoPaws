# Paws: Facial Features

## Eyes

- **Size**: Massive, glossy golden-amber eyes
- **Expression**: Large pupils that suggest a permanent state of wonder and curiosity
- **Impression**: Wide-eyed and inquisitive

## Markings

A unique series of orange "freckles" or spots bridge the nose and fan out across the cheeks, adding character and charm to Paws' face.

## Overall Appeal

These features combine to create a face that is expressive and endearing, perfectly supporting Paws' playful and curious nature.
