# Paws: Fur and Coloration

## Base Coat

The base coat is a pristine, fluffy white.

## Accent Stripes

Paws is heavily accented with bold, ginger-orange tabby stripes across the forehead and cheeks.

## Paint-Splatter Effect

Most distinctively, Paws is "splattered" with a kaleidoscope of colors:
- Teal
- Magenta
- Yellow
- Blue

These colors resemble flecks of wet paint or confetti embedded in the fur, creating a chaotic and vibrant aesthetic that reflects Paws' playful nature.
