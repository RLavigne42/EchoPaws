# Paws: Physicality

## Mohawk Tuft

Paws has a distinct "mohawk" tuft of fur between the ears, accented with the same rainbow hues found elsewhere (teal, magenta, yellow, and blue).

## Personality Through Design

This physical feature gives the character a slightly rebellious, spunky personality. Rather than a traditional cat silhouette, Paws' raised tuft and colorful accents suggest:
- A non-conformist attitude
- Youthful rebelliousness
- Constant movement and energy
- An artistic, creative spirit

## Overall Build

While described primarily through coloration and facial features, Paws' physicality emphasizes fluffy, expressive design that conveys perpetual motion and joy.
