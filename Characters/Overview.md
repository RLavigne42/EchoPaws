# Paws & Echo: Character Overview

## Introduction

Both Paws and Echo represent a striking contrast in energy—one bursting with chaotic color and the other radiating a calm, calculated serenity. This document provides a detailed breakdown of each character based on their visual design and thematic context.

## The Duo

- **Paws**: The Playful and Curious Cat
- **Echo**: The Wise Owl

Together, they form a complementary pair that embodies opposing forces of nature and personality.
