# Character Canon & Visual Specifications

This document consolidates all available character references for Echo and Paws, including personality traits, visual specs, and prompt-ready descriptions.

## Duo Overview

Echo and Paws represent opposing but complementary forces:

- **Echo**: symmetry, calm observation, and wisdom.
- **Paws**: vibrant chaos, curiosity, and energetic creativity.

## Echo (Wise Owl)

### Core Identity

- **Species**: Owl
- **Personality**: Wise, observant, serene
- **Visual Theme**: Cool tones, symmetry, ethereal precision
- **Energy**: Calm, measured, deliberate

### Facial Features

- **Eyes**: Deep indigo/galaxy blue; ancient, observant expression.
- **Beak**: Polished, blue-black, sharp and neat.
- **Ear tufts**: Swept-back, crown-like silhouette.

### Plumage & Texture

- **Palette**: White, silver, icy blue, deep navy.
- **Texture**: Scale-like feather plates on face/chest; armored yet elegant.
- **Linework**: Filigree-like detail on outer feathers.

### Demeanor Notes

- Conveys stillness, ancient wisdom, and regal dignity without arrogance.
- Symmetry and precision are central to the silhouette.

### Prompt-Ready Snapshot (Echo)

"Visualize Echo, a wise and serene owl avatar, centered in the frame facing the camera directly with a clear, white background to ensure no cropping. Echo's feathers are a sophisticated blend of deep blues and soft grays, with hints of silver that shimmer subtly. The owl's eyes are large, luminous, and filled with a depth of wisdom, directly engaging the viewer with a gentle, inviting gaze. Echo's posture is dignified and serene, embodying wisdom and insight."

## Paws (Playful Cat)

### Core Identity

- **Species**: Cat
- **Personality**: Playful, curious, energetic
- **Visual Theme**: Chaotic color, vibrant, rebellious
- **Energy**: Bursting with motion and joy

### Facial Features

- **Eyes**: Massive, glossy golden-amber; wide-eyed curiosity.
- **Markings**: Orange freckles/spotting across nose and cheeks.
- **Expression**: A soft, knowing smirk that reads mischievous and confident.

### Fur, Coloration, Physicality

- **Base coat**: Pristine, fluffy white.
- **Accent stripes**: Bold ginger-orange tabby stripes on forehead and cheeks.
- **Paint-splatter effect**: Teal, magenta, yellow, blue flecks.
- **Mohawk tuft**: Raised tuft between the ears with multicolor highlights.

### Prompt-Ready Snapshot (Paws)

"Imagine Paws, the playful and curious cat avatar, centered in the frame facing the camera directly with a clear, white background to ensure no cropping. Paws is a fluffy, whimsical feline with vibrant fur in shades of bright orange and white, accented with playful patterns. The cat's eyes are wide, sparkling with curiosity and mischief, inviting engagement and interaction. Paws' expression is lively and playful, with a slight smirk that hints at its adventurous spirit."

## Duo Interaction Notes

- Echo provides steadiness and analytical clarity.
- Paws injects creativity, spontaneity, and energy.
- Together they form a balanced, adaptive guide for users.
