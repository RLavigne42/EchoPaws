# World Bible & Setting Canon (Veridian Prism)

This document consolidates the setting lore, geography, narrative anchors, and symbol legend for the Veridian Prism.

## Core Premise

The Veridian Prism is a world where **Logic** and **Color** are fundamental forces. Echo embodies the logical, crystalline order, while Paws embodies creative chroma and kinetic change. The setting is designed to reinforce their partnership.

## Regions of the Veridian Prism

1. **The Silent Grove (Central Hub)**: balanced zone of mossy ruins and structural illusions.
2. **The Indigo Stillness (East)**: Echo’s influence; frozen waterfalls, slowed time.
3. **The Chroma-Fields (West)**: Paws’ influence; shifting prairie and chroma-storms.
4. **The Petrified Arches (North)**: limestone arches, navigation for Echo and guardians.
5. **The Silver Estuary (Coastal)**: liquid memory waters; origin of Silver Ferns.

## Silent Grove Scene Notes

- Widescreen, panoramic environment.
- High-contrast lighting: warm amber sunlight with indigo shadow.
- Symbolic of logic (Echo) and chaos (Paws) in a single environment.

## Narrative Anchor: “The Prism and the Parable”

- Paws jumps from a **Silver Fern** (memory-anchored illusion) and falls.
- Echo uses **Starlight Stillness** to slow time and rescue Paws.
- The duo’s synergy is defined: Echo prevents disaster; Paws prevents rigidity.

- **Path of Symmetry (Echo)**: sharp, blue-gray geometric routes.
- **Kinetic Shortcut (Paws)**: loose, multicolor splatters and arcs.

## Coordinate Zero: Heart of the Grove

**The Convergence Pillar**

- Translucent limestone pillar wrapped in Silver Ferns.
- Prism Well at the base reflecting kaleidoscopic teal/magenta/amber.
- Canopy of Stillness: leaves hover midair under Starlight Stillness.

**Synergy Mechanics**

1. Echo’s scale-feathers glow indigo.
2. Paws’ confetti flecks lift and mix with dust motes.
3. The duo plans journeys into the Fractured Wilds.

## Rendering Checklist (Identity Preservation)

- Oversized, glossy eyes for both characters.
- Maintain front-facing symmetry in identity reference images.
- Preserve Echo’s cool palette and Paws’ chroma splashes.
- Use 21:9 widescreen for duo scenes.
